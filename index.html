CASE FILE: THE MISSING AI PROTOTYPE

Incident Report

On 15 July 2026, the AIML Department was preparing for TechFest. A student team had developed an AI project called VisionGuard, which was scheduled to be demonstrated the next morning.

At 4:20 PM, the team discovered that the VisionGuard project folder had been deleted from Lab Workstation 03. The folder contained the final model, presentation slides, evaluation reports, and documentation.

Investigators collected witness statements, system logs, CCTV records, and other evidence. Your task is to determine who is responsible.

Persons of Interest

Rahul Mehta
AIML student. Worked on model training. Left the lab before 4 PM. Was not selected to present VisionGuard at TechFest.

Priya Shah
AIML student. Responsible for finalizing the presentation. Selected as the project presenter.

Aman Verma
AIML student. Not part of the VisionGuard team. Seen near the AIML corridor that afternoon.

Witness Statements

Campus preparations for TechFest had kept everyone busy that day.

However, several students noticed tension between Rahul and Priya after the presenter announcement.

Everyone knew only one student would represent the team on stage.

Corridor activity was unusually high throughout the afternoon.

Keeping track of who was where became difficult.

To some students, Aman seemed suspicious because he was repeatedly seen near the lab.

However, no one actually reported seeing him enter.

Earlier that afternoon, Rahul appeared frustrated after a discussion with teammates.

Given the circumstances, rumors spread quickly once the files disappeared.

A few students immediately suspected Aman.

Priya spent most of the afternoon preparing slides.

Several witnesses mentioned seeing her leave her desk briefly around 4 PM.
